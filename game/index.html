HOW TO PLAY

You are a fox spirit. Collect all 3 sacred orbs,
then slip through the torii gate EXIT unseen.

WASD / Arrow keys — move
Shift — sneak (slower, harder to spot, quieter)
Click — throw a pebble; guards investigate the CLANK
Bamboo thickets — hide inside, guards cannot see you
Rocks — block a guard's line of sight

Gold cone: patrolling.  Orange '?': suspicious.
Red '!': chasing — run and break line of sight!
If a guard touches you, you are CAUGHT.

P — pause    M — mute    Esc — back to menu
PRESS SPACE OR CLICK TO GO BACK
